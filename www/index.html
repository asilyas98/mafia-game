How to Play
🌙 Night Phase
Everyone passes the phone privately (or acts on their own device). Mafia kills. Doctor saves. Police investigates. Civilians make a blind guess.
☀️ Day Phase
Everyone discusses and votes. Majority needed to eliminate a suspect.
🏆 Win Conditions
Town wins — all Mafia eliminated.
Mafia wins — Mafia equals or outnumbers town.
Roles
🔴 Mafia — kills one person each night
🔵 Doctor — saves one person (including self)
🟡 Police — learns if their target is Mafia
🟢 Civilian — votes and reasons to survive
